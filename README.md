# 🧠 Overview

ClinNote AI is an AI-powered psychiatric documentation platform built to help mental health professionals create structured clinical notes within seconds.

The application is designed specifically for:

- Psychiatrists
- Therapists
- Psychologists
- Behavioral Health Professionals
- Counselors
- Clinical Social Workers

Mental health professionals spend a significant amount of time manually writing progress notes and clinical documentation. ClinNote AI reduces that burden by converting raw patient session information into professional, structured psychiatric notes instantly.

The platform currently supports:

- SOAP Notes
- DAP Notes
- BIRP Notes
- GIRP Notes

ClinNote AI focuses on:

✅ Faster documentation  
✅ Better workflow efficiency  
✅ Reduced clinician burnout  
✅ Consistent note formatting  
✅ AI-assisted psychiatric documentation

Mental health professionals often spend hours documenting therapy sessions, psychiatric evaluations, and progress notes.

# ✨ Features

# ⚡ AI-Powered Note Generation

Convert raw session summaries into structured psychiatric documentation instantly.

Example Input:

```txt
Patient reports increased anxiety over the last two weeks due to work stress. Sleep has been poor. Denies suicidal ideation. Therapist discussed coping strategies and grounding techniques.
```

Generated Output:

```txt
SOAP Note

Subjective:
Patient reports increased anxiety over the last two weeks related to occupational stress and poor sleep.

Objective:
Patient appeared mildly anxious but cooperative. Speech coherent and goal-directed.

Assessment:
Symptoms consistent with generalized anxiety exacerbation related to work stressors.

Plan:
Continue CBT interventions, practice grounding techniques, monitor sleep hygiene, follow up next session.
```

# 📝 Supported Clinical Documentation Formats

## 1. SOAP Notes

SOAP stands for:

- Subjective
- Objective
- Assessment
- Plan

Used widely in psychiatry, medicine, and therapy documentation.

---

## 2. DAP Notes

DAP stands for:

- Data
- Assessment
- Plan

Commonly used in psychotherapy and counseling.

---

## 3. BIRP Notes

BIRP stands for:

- Behavior
- Intervention
- Response
- Plan

Used for behavioral health treatment tracking.

---

## 4. GIRP Notes

GIRP stands for:

- Goals
- Intervention
- Response
- Plan

Focused on treatment goals and therapeutic progress.
